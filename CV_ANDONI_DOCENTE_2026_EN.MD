## About Me

Well, in these times where a CV can be quickly made through AI, I'm going to try to describe in my own words who I am and what I can contribute to teaching.

I have been working as an automation engineer (that is, a person who uses programming and automation tools in the most efficient way possible to solve a problem or highly repetitive and manual process) since 2021. I made my first attempt at this in 2017 at Deloitte, and it was something I quite enjoyed (it was my first job).

Since then, I have done several things (I worked as an electronics designer, I was in Rome working as an economic and commercial advisor with the BEINT scholarships from the Basque Government, I learned to program web applications, and finally I resumed my career as an automation engineer/programmer).

The latter led me to pursue a master's degree in teacher training at UNIR (note to the reader: this master's degree costs a lot of money, I wouldn't have done it if I didn't have a certain vocation or determination to be a teacher) while working at the same time.

Although I have done several things, they have all had a common denominator: **adding value and feeling useful**. Specifically, I feel very happy and fulfilled when I have had to explain or train others. I use active learning methodology with students (that is, making it as practical as possible) especially through challenges, without this entailing a detriment to the theoretical foundations of the subject (although this is sometimes utopian, especially when time is limited).
